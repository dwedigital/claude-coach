## Plan Overview

| Phase | Weeks | Focus |
|-------|-------|-------|
| Base | W1-4 | Aerobic base, easy volume, weekly long run to 20 km |
| Build | W5-8 | Threshold + tempo, long run to 28 km, marathon-pace touches |
| Peak | W9-10 | Marathon-pace long runs, 2× 20k @ MP inside the long run |
| Taper | W11-12 | Volume drops 30-50%, quality preserved, race day |

**Mesocycle pattern:** 3:1 (3 weeks build, 1 week recovery). Applied to W1-4, W5-8, W9-12.

**Weekly volume ceiling:** never jump more than 10% week-on-week. Cap at ~90 km/week for a first-time sub-3:30 attempt.

## Race-Day Brief

### Pacing anchors

| Segment | Pace | Cue |
|---------|------|-----|
| km 0-5 | MP + 5-10 sec | "Ease into it — feel out of breath at km 3, you went out too fast" |
| km 5-32 | MP | "Cruise" |
| km 32-38 | MP or slightly slower | "Manage" |
| km 38-42 | Whatever's left | "Empty the tank" |

### Fuelling

- **R-3 to R-1:** carb loading, 7-10 g/kg/day
- **Race morning:** breakfast 2.5-3 h before gun. ~1 g/kg carb. Coffee.
- **Pre-race:** 1 gel 15 min before start
- **In-race:** gel every 30 min from km 10 (5-6 gels total). Water at every aid station, sports drink at 3/4 of them.

### Kit

- Race shoes (broken in with 30-50 km on them)
- HR strap + watch
- Race number belt
- Anti-chafe balm (nipples, inner thighs)
- Vaseline on toes
- Weather-appropriate layers (throwaway warmups if cold)
