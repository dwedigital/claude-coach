# Playbook: Triathlon

Sport-specific extensions to the core coaching methodology for swim/bike/run athletes.

## Session types

- **Brick** (bike → run): transition practice, teaches legs to run off the bike. At least once per week during build phase.
- **Discipline-specific technique**: swim drills, run form work, bike cadence drills — lower intensity, skill focus.
- **Open water swim**: sighting practice, race-kit rehearsal. Non-negotiable before an open-water race.
- **Race-pace brick**: full-distance rehearsal in the final build week (dress rehearsal).

## Discipline priorities

- **Swim:** technique over volume. Include drill work every session. Swim is where most triathletes lose time to poor efficiency, not poor fitness. Focus areas: head position, high-elbow catch, breathing pattern (every 3 for open water sighting).
- **Bike:** where the biggest time gains are available. Long rides build aerobic base. Practise race nutrition on the bike — the stomach needs training too.
- **Run:** most injury-prone discipline. Conservative volume increases, prioritise consistency over speed. Off-the-bike pace is 20-40 sec/km slower than standalone pace at the same effort.

## Zone anchoring — where the numbers come from

| Discipline | Primary anchor | Secondary |
|-----------|----------------|-----------|
| Run | HR (from lab test or 5k benchmark) | Pace/km |
| Bike | Power (FTP-anchored zones) | HR |
| Swim | Pace/100m (CSS-anchored) | RPE |

Bike HR runs **5-10 bpm lower** than running HR at the same perceived effort — the muscle recruitment differs. Don't compare HR across disciplines.

## Transitions (T1, T2)

Practise in training. Lay out kit as you would race-day, rehearse the sequence. Sub-90s T1 and sub-60s T2 are realistic for sprint/Olympic distance with practice.

- **T1 sequence:** wetsuit strip → helmet ON before touching bike → run bike out → mount over the line → clip in
- **T2 sequence:** dismount before the line → run bike to rack → helmet OFF → shoes on → GO

## Race-pace targeting by distance

| Distance | Swim intensity | Bike intensity | Run intensity |
|----------|---------------|----------------|---------------|
| Super Sprint (400/10/2.5) | RPE 8-9 hard | Z4 threshold | Z4-Z5 |
| Sprint (750/20/5) | RPE 7-8 | Z3-Z4 (85-95% FTP) | Z4 |
| Olympic (1500/40/10) | RPE 6-7 | Z3 (75-85% FTP) | Z3-Z4 |
| 70.3 (1900/90/21) | RPE 5-6 | Z2-Z3 (70-80% FTP) | Z3 |
| IM (3800/180/42) | RPE 4-5 | Z2 (65-75% FTP) | Z2-Z3 |

## Race-week protocol

- **R-7 to R-4:** taper begins. Volume ~50% of build week. Keep 1-2 short race-pace touches to preserve sharpness.
- **R-3:** race-eve or two-days-before shakeout run. Openers on the bike (20 min easy + 3x3 min race-pace).
- **R-1:** full rest. Kit lay-out. Carb-up dinner. Bed early.
- **R-day:** wake T-2:30h. Light breakfast + coffee. Warm-up T-45 min. Pre-race gel T-15 min. Race.

## Common mistakes

- **Swimming too little.** Skill-limited discipline, and skill takes reps. Sub-2 sessions/week and technique doesn't change.
- **Not practising nutrition on the bike.** Race-day GI issues almost always come from untested race-day nutrition. Test everything in training.
- **Running off the bike too fast.** The legs feel wooden for the first 1-2 km off the bike. Race-pace kicks in from km 2, not km 1.
- **Skipping the dress rehearsal.** The final build week's full-distance brick is non-negotiable for time projection and equipment testing.

## Race-day cues by leg

**Swim:** hard from gun, no panic, sight every 6 strokes on open water.
**Bike:** race-pace power from T1+2 min. Fuel every 30 min. Don't chase surges.
**Run:** hold race pace through km 2 (until legs come alive). Empty the tank in the last 20% of the distance.
