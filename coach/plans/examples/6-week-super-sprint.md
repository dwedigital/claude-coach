## Race-Day Brief

### Pacing anchors per leg

| Leg | Distance | Target | Cue |
|-----|----------|--------|-----|
| 🏊 Swim | 400m OW | ~8 min, RPE 8-9 hard but controlled | "Hard from gun, no panic, sight every 6 strokes" |
| ⚡ T1 | — | sub-90s | Helmet ON, run, mount, clip |
| 🚴 Bike | 10km | ~17 min, race-pace power, HR 160+ | "Race the bike — sprint racing rewards aggression" |
| ⚡ T2 | — | sub-45s | Helmet OFF, shoes on, GO |
| 🏃 Run | 2.5km | ~11 min, race pace, HR 165-175 | "Hold race pace through km 2. Empty the tank last 500m" |

### Morning timeline (anchored to T = swim start)

- T -2:30h: Wake. Light breakfast + coffee.
- T -1:30h: Arrive. Register if not done Sat. Set up T1 + T2.
- T -0:45h: Warm-up: 5 min jog + 4x10s strides + dynamic mobility.
- T -0:15h: Wetsuit on. 1x gel pre-race (race too short for in-race fuel).
- T 0: 🏁 SWIM START.
