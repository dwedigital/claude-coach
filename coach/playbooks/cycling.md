# Playbook: Cycling

Sport-specific extensions for cyclists (fondos, TTs, stage races, gravel, road).

## Session types

- **Endurance** (Z2, 65-75% FTP): long steady-state rides. The bedrock. 60-70% of weekly time.
- **Sweet spot** (Z3, 85-95% FTP): 2× 20 min or 3× 15 min sustained. Highest bang-per-hour training zone for FTP gains.
- **Threshold** (Z4, 95-105% FTP): 4× 8 min or 2× 20 min at FTP. Direct FTP work.
- **VO2max** (Z5, 106-120% FTP): 5× 3 min or 5× 4 min all-out. Grows the top of the aerobic ceiling.
- **Hill repeats**: race-specific for climb-heavy events. 5-10 min hard on climb, easy back.
- **Cadence drills**: 3× 5 min high-cadence (>100 rpm) low-torque; 3× 5 min low-cadence (60-70) high-torque. Neuromuscular.

## Zone anchoring

**FTP is the anchor.** Test every 6-8 weeks:
- **20-min test:** 20 min all-out steady, FTP = avg watts × 0.95
- **Ramp test:** graded until failure — quick but produces slightly higher numbers than 20-min

Once you have FTP, zones fall out:

| Zone | Name | Power | % FTP | Purpose |
|------|------|-------|-------|---------|
| Z1 | Recovery | <55% | Active recovery, spin |
| Z2 | Endurance | 55-75% | Base, long rides |
| Z3 | Tempo / sweet-spot | 76-90% | Race-pace endurance |
| Z4 | Threshold | 91-105% | FTP work |
| Z5 | VO2max | 106-120% | Top-end |
| Z6+ | Anaerobic / neuromuscular | 121%+ | Sprints, attacks |

HR is a secondary check — useful for spotting cardiac drift, fatigue, environmental load (heat).

## Discipline-specific priorities

| Event | Peak session | Weekly hours |
|-------|-------------|--------------|
| Fondo (100+ km) | 4-5h Z2 with sweet-spot blocks | 8-12 h |
| Time trial (40k+) | 2× 20 min @ 100-105% FTP | 8-14 h |
| Stage race | Consecutive-day back-to-back long rides | 12-20 h |
| Gravel / MTB endurance | Long Z2 with technical repeats | 6-12 h |
| Track / criterium | Sprints + anaerobic capacity | 8-12 h |

## Wheels + kit for race day

Match wheel choice to course:
- **Flat + windy:** shallow (45mm) for crosswind stability
- **Flat + calm:** deep (60mm+) for aero
- **Climbing:** shallow, light
- **Mixed:** 40-50mm all-rounders

Tyre pressure matters more than most people think. Modern low-pressure model (Silca / Zipp):

| Rider weight | 28mm tyre | 32mm tyre |
|--------------|-----------|-----------|
| 60 kg | F 42 / R 47 | F 38 / R 42 |
| 70 kg | F 46 / R 51 | F 42 / R 46 |
| 80 kg | F 50 / R 55 | F 45 / R 50 |
| 90 kg | F 54 / R 59 | F 49 / R 54 |

Drop 3 psi for rough surfaces, add 3 for pure smooth. **Hookless wheels max 72.5 psi.** Never exceed.

## Fuelling

- **Endurance rides (2h+):** 60-80g carb/hour from drink mix + gels/bars. Start at 30 min, don't wait for hunger.
- **Race day (fondo / TT):** 90-120g carb/hour possible with training. Requires stomach training over weeks — don't try untested race-day.
- **Sweet-spot / threshold sessions (<90 min):** water + optional carb drink. No solid fuel needed.
- **Recovery rides:** water only. Fuel from meals.

## Recovery

- **Cardiac drift** during a Z2 ride indicates dehydration, glycogen depletion, or accumulated fatigue. Watch avg HR at same power.
- **Bike is less injury-prone than running** but overtraining still bites. Signs: reduced peak power, elevated morning HR, mood swings.
- **Rest days are optional on the bike** if the previous days were true Z2 — but true Z2 is harder than most amateurs think.

## Common mistakes

- **Sweet-spotting all your Z2 rides.** Z2 must be genuinely easy — 65-75% FTP with a conversational HR. Otherwise the aerobic base doesn't build.
- **Skipping cadence work.** Fixed low cadence (<80) recruits fast-twitch fibres unnecessarily and grinds down knees.
- **Not testing FTP regularly.** Zones drift 3-8% per training block. Working from stale zones underdoses the session.
- **Under-fuelling long rides.** The classic bonk at 3h is entirely preventable with 60g/h carbs from minute 30.
- **Chasing Strava segments on easy days.** Turns the plan to mush. Easy is easy.
