## Race-Day Brief

### Pacing

| Segment | Target power / effort | Cue |
|---------|----------------------|-----|
| First 30 min | 65-75% FTP (Z2) | "Don't chase the surges. Save it" |
| Middle bulk | 75-85% FTP (Z2-Z3) | "This is the money zone" |
| Climbs | Up to 105% FTP for <5 min | "Steady grinding on longer climbs" |
| Final 25% | Whatever's left | "Empty the tank on the last flat / final climb" |

### Fuelling

- **60-90 g carb/h** — mix of drink mix (~60g/bottle) + gels + bar
- **Real food by 90 min mark** — bar/banana/flapjack before stomach gets sensitive
- **Water:** 500-750 ml/h in mild weather, more in heat
- **Start at 30 min.** Don't wait for hunger.

### Kit

- **Wheels:** shallow (45mm) if windy/hilly, deep (60mm+) if flat/calm
- **Tyres:** 32mm at F 48 / R 52 psi baseline (adjust per Cycling playbook)
- **Nutrition:** 6-8 gels, 1-2 bars, drink mix for at least 3 bottles
- **Backup:** spare tube + CO2 or plug kit, multitool, phone
- **Weather kit:** rain jacket if forecast <60% dry
